1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your device when you visit them. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, provide a better user experience, and gather information about how the site is used.

Cookies can be:

  • First-party cookies: Set directly by our website
  • Third-party cookies: Set by services we integrate with (such as Google Analytics, LinkedIn, Meta)
  • Session cookies: Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser
  • Persistent cookies: Remain on your device for a set period

2. Why We Use Cookies

  • To make our Site function properly and securely
  • To remember your preferences and settings
  • To understand how visitors interact with our Site
  • To improve our Site's performance and user experience
  • To deliver relevant advertising and marketing
  • To measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns
  • To prevent fraud and enhance security
